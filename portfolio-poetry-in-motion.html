Multimodal Poetry in Motion

Turning the Montserrat volcanic crisis and original student poetry into a multimodal digital book — grounded in real community history, built neuro-inclusive from the ground up.

Home / Projects / Multimodal Poetry in Motion
Case Study · KPS Book Creator Project, Montserrat

From blank-page paralysis to published, multimodal authorship.

Challenge

Secondary school students needed to engage deeply with the complex, lived history of the Montserrat volcanic crisis and original poetry without facing learning or technical friction. Traditional curriculum delivery often creates barriers — blank-page paralysis, spelling anxiety, reading speed, cognitive overload — that shut neurodivergent learners out of rigorous literary analysis and creative digital publishing.

Solution

Techembrace designed The Bridge V2.0, a neuro-inclusive access layer integrated with a structured six-stage Book Creator poetry workflow ("Poetry in Motion"). Grounded in the historical foundation of educator Gertrude Shotte and the childhood poetry of Terrez Thomas, the project guides students to transform text into a multimodal digital book — combining poem text, audio narration, AI-generated images, and 10-second videos. The Bridge eliminates barriers without lowering standards, using a Dual-Track Studio Method, prompt frames, chunked tasks, and spoken-reflection options.

Result

Students became ethical digital authors through the "Meaning Before Media" principle and the 40/20/40 ethical AI model (40% human intent, 20% AI generation, 40% human curation). Every learner demonstrated high-level critical judgement, documented their creative choices in a running AI Usage Log, and published a final multimodal portfolio that preserves Montserratian student voices across generations.

Service Pillars

This project draws on Pillar 02 — Immersive Learning & Experience Design, Pillar 03 — Instructional Design & Digital Curriculum, and Pillar 06 — AI Creative, Visual & Audio Strategy.
