Overview

From sceptic to practitioner.

Technology rollouts fail at the point of adoption, not procurement. This pillar is about the people side of transformation — diagnosing where your teams actually are, and building the governance and CPD pathway that moves them forward safely.

The Detail

Focus, measurement, and moat.

Focus

GAT-M governance implementation, the Five AI Personas diagnostic, GDPR-aligned compliance, and tiered CPD pathways.

RoL Metric

Staff AI-tool adoption and confidence scores tracked pre- and post-programme, plus a measurable drop in shadow-AI usage.

Moat Contribution

A workforce that can use AI safely and confidently is a compounding advantage no competitor can buy off the shelf.

Execution & Expertise

Change management and CPD delivery experience across further education and enterprise workforces.

Key Activities

What this looks like in practice.

  • GAT-M governance framework implementation
  • Five AI Personas diagnostic
  • GDPR-aligned AI compliance review
  • Tiered CPD pathway design
  • Change management & adoption coaching
  • Workshop facilitation & team enablement
How We Deliver It

Three named workshop tracks.

Track 1

AI Foundations

For teams new to AI tools — building baseline confidence, safe usage habits, and dispelling the fear that keeps adoption stuck.

Track 2

AI in Practice

For teams ready to embed AI into daily workflows — role-specific use cases, prompt literacy, and governance in action.

Track 3

AI Leadership

For managers and leads — how to govern, evaluate, and scale AI use across a team responsibly.
