How we bring ThingLink into learning

Immersive, inclusive and built for staff to adopt.

Bring immersive learning into your setting without a new hardware budget or a steep learning curve. We design it so every learner can take part from the first draft.

Use the devices you already have

ThingLink runs in a web browser, so scenes can open on the laptops, tablets and screens your learners already use. We plan each build around the kit in your setting, not new hardware.

Simple for staff to adopt

We offer short, practical CPD sessions and reusable templates, so your staff can build their own scenes instead of waiting for us.

Inclusive from the first draft

We apply Universal Design for Learning from the start: clear routes through each scene, plain-language labels, and text or audio alternatives for key content.

Student creativity and ownership

Learners can build their own scenes and showcases, so they become makers as well as viewers.

Skills for work and life

Building a scene develops digital, writing and presentation skills. On vocational courses, a 360° scene lets learners explore a real workplace before they step into it.
