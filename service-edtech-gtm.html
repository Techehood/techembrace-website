Overview

Strategy before scale.

Scaling the wrong thing faster is not a strategy. Before a platform, product, or pilot goes to market, this pillar stress-tests the model — the audit and the architecture that make growth defensible rather than fragile.

The Detail

Focus, measurement, and moat.

Focus

Digital audits, MVP validation, AI procurement guidance, and go-to-market architecture for EdTech founders and platform teams.

RoL Metric

Time-to-validated-MVP and reduction in wasted procurement/build spend on the wrong AI tooling before scale.

Moat Contribution

A go-to-market architecture built on your actual data and users, not a generic playbook — harder for competitors to replicate because it's yours.
