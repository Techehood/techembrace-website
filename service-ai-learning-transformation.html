Overview

"Build the moat, not just the tools."

Where to Start

The Hero Use Case Selection Matrix

Not every AI opportunity is worth building first. We map candidate use cases against two axes — business impact and implementation feasibility — to find the "hero" use case that proves value fast and builds momentum for everything that follows.

Quick Wins

High feasibility, lower impact. Good for building internal confidence early.

Hero Use Cases

High feasibility, high impact. This is where we start — visible proof, fast.

Strategic Bets

Lower feasibility, high impact. Roadmapped once the foundation is proven.

Deprioritise

Lower feasibility, lower impact. Parked until conditions change.
