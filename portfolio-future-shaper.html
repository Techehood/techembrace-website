AI Creators — The Future Shaper Course

A 3-year curriculum that teaches logic and architecture, not just code. Built for neurodivergent learners first — which makes it better for everyone.

Home / Projects / AI Creators — The Future Shaper Course
Case Study · Instructional Design — Standalone Deep Dive

Curriculum design that stands on its own.

Challenge

Most AI and coding education for young people teaches surface-level tool use — prompts and outputs — without building the underlying logic and systems thinking that make someone a creator rather than a consumer of AI. Almost none of it is designed for neurodivergent learners from the outset.

Solution

We designed The Future Shaper Course: a 3-year spiral curriculum built on a Prompt-to-Product pipeline that takes learners from a single prompt through to a finished, shippable product — revisiting core concepts at increasing depth each year rather than teaching them once and moving on. Every unit is built UDL-first and neurodiversity-affirming from the ground up, not adapted afterward.

Result

A standalone proof that Techembrace's Instructional Design capability extends beyond a single sector — the same UDL-first, neurodiversity-affirming design principles validated at TeamChefs.tv, applied here to build tomorrow's AI creators, not just today's AI users.

Design Principles

What makes the curriculum spiral.

Prompt-to-Product Pipeline

Learners move from a single prompt to a finished, shippable product — practising the full arc of AI-assisted creation, not just the prompt itself.

3-Year Spiral Architecture

Core concepts are revisited at increasing depth each year, building logic and systems thinking rather than one-off tool familiarity.

UDL by Design

Universal Design for Learning principles are built into every unit from the outset — not retrofitted for accessibility after the fact.

Neurodivergent Learners First

Designed for neurodivergent learners as the starting point — a design choice that makes the course more effective for every learner.
