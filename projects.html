Case Study · Strategy & Training

TeamChefs.tv Learning Ecosystem

Challenge

A hospitality training provider needed a way to deliver, track, and assess culinary curriculum at scale — with instant, professional-level feedback that a small team couldn't provide manually.

Solution

We designed a custom Learning Management System for TeamChefs.tv and built an AI assessment engine that integrates large language models directly into the feedback loop, giving learners instant, professional-level responses on uploaded work.

Result

A connected data and talent system that turns fragmented curriculum delivery into a compounding capability — measurable growth in shared organisational knowledge that competitors can't simply buy.

Case Study · AI Creative

AI Creative for Brands & Culture

Challenge

Creative clients (including imagine.Art and Outlandish Music) needed distinctive visual and audio assets that generic AI output couldn't deliver — work platforms would actually recognise.

Solution

We applied AI creative direction across image, motion, and mood-board production — using ethically sourced generative models with a strong, brand-true point of view rather than default AI aesthetics.

Result

Tangible, high-value creative assets delivered at a speed generic agencies couldn't match, proving Techembrace's AI Creative & Visual Strategy pillar in market — a space with zero direct competitor coverage.

Case Study · VR/AR & Digital Heritage

Virtual Croydon 360°

Challenge

A cultural heritage initiative needed to preserve and present physical spaces and public history in a way that reached people who could never visit in person.

Solution

We built a 360° immersive platform using ThingLink and real-location photography, letting users click hotspots to explore heritage sites and connect them to learning content.

Result

A validated Immersive Learning & Experience Design capability — applying VR/AR technology to cultural heritage preservation and public engagement in a space where competitor coverage is effectively zero.
