Before you buy anything

Three tests we use before we buy

  1. Does it solve a job we already do every week? If we cannot name the job, we do not buy.
  2. Can we leave? We check our content exports cleanly, so a vendor closing never traps our work.
  3. Is there a refund window? AppSumo offers a 60-day money-back promise, so we test on real work first.

A lifetime deal depends on the vendor surviving. That is why the second test matters most.
