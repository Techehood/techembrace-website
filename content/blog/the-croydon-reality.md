_The decision happens on the phone — not at the door._

Across Croydon, independent retailers and small customer-facing businesses are losing visibility without realising it.

Not because they're not working hard. Most owners are working _very_ hard — on the shop floor, on the till, on the orders. The problem is that the customer decision now happens somewhere else: on a phone, before the customer ever walks past the door.

## What the numbers actually say

- **67% of customers check a business online before walking through the door.**
- **46% of all Google searches are local** — "near me," "open now," "best in Croydon."
- **A Google Business Profile with photos gets 42% more direction requests** than one without.
- **75% of customers never scroll past the first page** of search results.
- **A one-star rating difference moves local revenue by 5–9%.**
- **Businesses that respond to reviews see 35% more customer engagement.**

_Sources: BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey; Google Local Search behaviour data; Moz Local Search Ranking Factors._

## The gap isn't effort. It's evidence.

Most owners don't know what their presence actually looks like to a customer searching from a phone in Croydon on a Saturday afternoon.

A new takeaway in Croydon — good food, launch discount running.                                             You check Google. The address is right, but the 360° view is of the old shop, the photos are blurry, and the last review is from 2019.

**Do you go?**

They don't know how their Google Business Profile compares to the three competitors within walking distance. They don't know which social account a potential customer sees first—or whether it's been updated this quarter. They don't know what their website looks like on the phone the customer is actually holding.

That's not a failure. It's a blind spot— and one you can close.

## What a diagnostic actually does

A proper digital presence audit doesn't tell a business owner they're doing it wrong. It tells them, with evidence:

- **Where** they're being found (and where they're not).
- **Who** is being found instead — the competitors capturing searches they should be winning.
- **Why** — the specific gaps in profile, website, social, and review activity that are costing visibility.
- **What to do first** — prioritised by impact, not by what's easiest.

That evidence is the thing. Without it, every marketing spend is a guess. With it, the next move is obvious.

## The Croydon context

Croydon is a competitive local market. Independent retailers compete not just with each other but with national chains, out-of-town retail parks, and online-only sellers. The businesses that win locally aren't always the ones with the biggest budgets—they're the ones who understand what their customers see before they decide where to go.
