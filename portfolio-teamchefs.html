The AI-Powered Culinary Feedback Loop

Instructors were buried under 20 minutes of manual feedback per dish. We built an AI feedback loop that critiques plating like a 5-star Michelin chef — instantly.

Home / Projects / The AI-Powered Culinary Feedback Loop
Case Study · Teamchefs.tv

From 20 minutes of feedback to instant, structured critique.

Challenge

Vocational culinary students needed to master plating precision and spatial technique, but instructors were buried — roughly 20 minutes of manual feedback per student, per dish, every assessment.

Solution

Students used Adobe Express and Canva Pro to AI-mock up plating concepts before cooking, then uploaded photos of the finished dish to myChatGPT — the custom Teamchefs.tv engine, prompted to critique like a 5-star Michelin chef — for immediate, structured feedback on colour, consistency, and geometry.

Result

A connected feedback loop that turns fragmented, instructor-bottlenecked assessment into instant, professional-level critique at scale.

From Classroom to WorldSkills

When Adobe Express shipped Firefly's generative AI to the public on 16 August 2023, Techembrace was already building the training pipeline to put it in students' hands. Within the 2023–24 academic year, the myChatGPT feedback loop and Adobe Express plating workflow were rolled out to Professional Cookery cohorts across two campuses and both year groups — not a single pilot classroom, but a repeatable curriculum.

The results reached beyond the classroom. WorldSkills’ Catering: Foundation Skills category — created specifically to showcase the abilities of students with SEND, additional learning needs, or who require additional support for learning, tested against core competencies at Entry Level 3 or Level 1 — is exactly the population this neuroinclusive, visual-first training pipeline was built for. Under Daniel’s coaching, students reached the quarter-finals in both 2023 and 2025, and a London FE college continues to enter students in this category every year.
