Overview

"Don't describe it. Take them there."

Some things can't be taught from a slide. Risk, scale, empathy, history — they need to be experienced. This pillar builds the immersive environments that make abstract or hard-to-reach concepts tangible and memorable.

The Detail

Focus, measurement, and moat.

Focus

VR/AR training simulations, 360° environments, and digital heritage preservation built for Meta Quest and ThingLink.

RoL Metric

Retention and completion rates versus traditional training formats, plus reduced incident/error rates where the experience simulates real-world risk.

Moat Contribution

A library of proprietary immersive assets that competitors relying on generic e-learning templates simply do not have.

Execution & Expertise

Delivery experience spanning cultural heritage preservation projects and enterprise VR/AR training builds.
