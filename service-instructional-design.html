Overview

Fast content doesn't teach anyone anything.

AI can generate content in seconds. It cannot generate learning. This pillar is where instructional design discipline — Universal Design for Learning, neurodiversity-first thinking, and outcomes-led structure — meets AI-accelerated production, without sacrificing either.

Executive Briefing

The Edge Effect: Designed for the Hardest Case, Built for Everyone

In ecology, the boundary where two ecosystems meet is the most productive ground there is. We design the same way — for dyslexic, ADHD, and visual-first minds — and the friction we remove for them turns out to be friction everyone was carrying. High-contrast, hierarchically mapped layouts, and complex work broken into 15-minute micro-tasks: every design constraint that serves a neurodivergent learner also strips the clutter for the person working next to them. (Note: this section paraphrases Daniel's own dyslexia/ADHD story as a draft — swap in his exact words when he's ready.)
